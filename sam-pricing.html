Questions

Can't find what you need? Ask your counselor to reach out.

Is Explorer really free forever?

Yes. Finding opportunities near you should never cost money. Achiever adds tracking and alerts for students who want extra help organizing their portfolio.

Will SAM do my homework for me?

No, on purpose. SAM explains concepts, points to trustworthy sources and asks guiding questions, but it won't give final answers to assignments.

Where do the opportunities come from?

SAM gathers public listings from volunteer boards, parks & rec departments, sports clubs and learning platforms, then filters them by your location, radius, interests and schedule.

Do I need a parent's permission to sign up?

Students under 13 need a parent or guardian to approve the account. We only ask for your ZIP code, not your home address.

What if my family can't afford Achiever?

Apply for a free Achiever pass from your account settings. No paperwork, just a short form. Title I schools also get SAM free.
